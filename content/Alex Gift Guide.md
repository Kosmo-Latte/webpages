## Introduction
Thank you for thinking about getting a gift for me! I'm eternally grateful for every gesture of generosity and **anything** given is received with gratitude. Writing this is a little awkward for me - so I have used third person throughout, as it lets me have a little bit of intellectual distance from the silliness of it. I also apologise for being quite so hard to buy for!

I've laid this guide out with three categories, depending on quite how much info you want at once:

**General Ideas** - guidelines, themes, yeses and nos. If you want to get some supporting ideas without explicit examples, this section is ideal.

**Examples** - Referring to the above, some examples of similar (possibly already owned) items which can inform purchases, but not explicit lists. 

**Wish Lists** - These are explicit lists of things I would want, but adjacent gifts are also welcome! There are also more expensive items on here, but those aren't expected - and things that are related would be great too!

Thanks,

Alex! 💜

## I Want General Ideas
### General Yes and No
#### Yes Please

**Coffee, savouries, and sweets.** Favourites include:
- Unusual coffees (unique processes or limited run single-estate, less so blends)
- White chocolate (but not Lindor, or Milkybar)
- Fruit flavours (but not strawberry or kiwi)
- Rarer sauces, condiments, or additives - think artisanal hot sauce, world foods which are more obscure and fun, etc.  
- Scottish treats (only available in Scotland) e.g. tablet, Irn Bru flavours they don't do south of the border, Edinburgh rock, etc.
- Dutch treats e.g. kruidnoten, pepernoten, drop (salty licorice) - especially half-and-half styles but not double salt, and coffee candies.
- Other worldwide treats! New things are always interesting to try - Alex has enjoyed special items from friends from across South America, the USA, Europe, and Asia!

**Gifts related to your interests, or your culture, or where you live.** Anything from your hometown that you think would be especially intriguing or interesting - or an aspect of your hobby that can be shared - are very gratefully received! This absolutely applies to anything handmade.

#### No Thank You

**Utilitarian Gifts** - i.e., items which are purely useful for their practical utility, like a travel bag, desk organiser, water bottle, etc. Alex is *incredibly* picky about daily-use items, so they prefer things which have a more personal flair from the giver, or are more directly towards their aesthetic interests!

**Clothes**, *except* accessories like hats (Alex has a large head), gloves (and wide hands), and socks (size 10 wide, please).  Definitely no underwear, outerwear, or things like jackets/etc. Losing weight means these might simply end up being given away again, which would be unfair.

**Alcohol,** except small amounts of unusual alcohol; Alex simply doesn't drink often enough or in large enough quantities to reliably get through a full bottle of spirits. Something obscure and fun is good to share, though! 

Jewellery which is made of **pewter**, **wood**, or **plastic**. These are a bit more fragile and not quite as nice, unless they're very very specific. 

**Beard-care Items** - this is hopefully getting shaved off soon!

### Gift Themes
#### Jewellery 
- **Sun and Moon** - explicitly the sun, not star & moon. 
- **Arcane/occult/magical symbology**, particularly classical rather than modern (i.e. goetic, demonological, druidic, etc)
- **Lynxes** (ideally Eurasian Lynxes), but absolutely not real animal products like fur/fangs/claws etc. 
- **Geometric & industrial themes** - minimalist, simple shapes, Bauhaus styling, etc.
- **Gothic or macabre symbols or styles**; blades, thorned roses, etc. Nothing too over the top - small and subtle works fine.
- **Non-binary or Bisexual Motifs** (the subtler the better, for safety)

#### Clothing - Accessories
 - Nothing inherently excessively masculine, like common scally brands (North Face, Adidas, Nike etc) A pop of colour is nice - less so pastels, though. Practical and plain is fine for things like gloves/woolly hats etc.
 - Similar motifs from the jewellery section, but potentially more subtle (like the Day of the Dead scarf I own - lots of small colourful skulls making one big pattern, but not too obviously skull-shaped at first glance)

#### Fragrances (for cosmetics etc)
**Yes**
- Citrus in general
- Good coffee
- Vanilla
- Spices (cinnamon, cardamom, etc)
- Floral but more complex


**No**
- "Traditional masculine scents" i.e. fresh soap, leather, musk, tobacco, etc
- Super sweet sickly fruit i.e. strawberries
- Floral as in *flowery* - e.g. roses
